BWSTL Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

This app collects no data.

BWSTL does not collect, transmit, store remotely, sell, or share any personal data, usage data, analytics, or identifiers. There is no network access, no account system, no third-party SDKs, and no tracking of any kind. The app does not use App Tracking Transparency because it does not track users across apps or websites.

What stays on your device

Your preferences (theme, viewport settings, shape parameters, saved shape library) are stored locally using standard OS preference storage and are never transmitted anywhere. Files you choose to export are written only to locations you select (or, on macOS, your Downloads folder), and go nowhere else.

Applies to

BWSTL for macOS, iPadOS, and iOS — Free and Paid editions.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the “Last updated” date above will change with it.
